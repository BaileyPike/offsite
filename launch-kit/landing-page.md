### The problem (mirror their language back to them)

You already know the symptoms:

- Marketing reports 400 MQLs. Sales says 60 are worth calling. Nobody agrees on what the data means.
- Your forecast swings on anecdote because top-of-funnel coverage is thin and mid-funnel execution is inconsistent.
- Your CRM is roughly 80% noise, and every AI initiative you bolt on top inherits that rot.

### What this actually is

GTM, Rebuilt is a practitioner-level newsletter for people who run revenue, not people who sell software to people who run revenue. Every issue is built around one question: **is your revenue engine coherent enough for AI to actually help — and if not, what do you fix first?**

**Four things I write about, every week:**

1. **Human + agent org design** — how CRO orgs are actually restructuring roles and headcount around AI agents (not the vendor pitch version)
2. **Pipeline & forecast integrity in the AI era** — data rot, lead scoring, handoff failures, and what fixes them
3. **Myth-busting AI GTM claims** — a real teardown of what's working vs. what's hype, with the numbers
4. **The CRO survival guide** — how to prove revenue impact inside a tenure window that's shrinking every year

---

### What this isn't

- Not a tool roundup. I don't get paid to feature vendors, and I won't.
- Not a strategy deck. If a piece doesn't change what you do on Monday, it doesn't run.
- Not for CMOs looking for brand/demand-gen content, or PLG growth folks — plenty of good newsletters cover that ground. This one is written for the people who own the number.
